Research department

Apply to the research track

Our systematic research department recruits in small cohorts on a rolling basis. Members work in pods designing, backtesting, and running strategies on simulated accounts.

Who should apply

Coders and serious analysts. What matters is that you can code, take analysis seriously, and will show up to build.

How it works

Apply → review → short conversation → join the next cohort.

What we expect

Consistent participation in your pod, honest backtesting, and engagement with the society.

FAQ

FAQ

Do I need trading experience?

No. For general membership, no technical background is required at all. For the research track we care about analytical thinking and programming ability, not trading experience.

How much time does MQR require?

Membership requires nothing beyond showing up to what interests you. The research track is 2–10 hours per week for pod work; competition weeks can require more.

Is MQR for Maastricht University students only?

Primarily, yes. Reach out if you are at a nearby institution.

Is any real money involved?

All trading is simulated on paper accounts, no real money is involved.

Why sponsor MQR

MQR reaches technically-minded students across data science, AI, econometrics, mathematics, and computer science. Those are the profiles quant firms actually recruit. Our members choose to spend their free time on this.

What you get

Named event sponsorship for workshops, competitions, and datathons. Speaking and workshop slots on campus. Logo placement on our site, socials, and association attire. Direct access to members for internship and graduate recruitment.
