How to apply

01

General member

Access to all workshops, sessions, insights, competitions, and socials. Open to any UM student with an interest in quantitative finance. No coding or trading experience required.

02

Committee

Help run the association. Committee members take on events, sponsors, social media, and public relations.

03

Research department

Work in small pods to design, backtest, and run systematic strategies on simulated capital. For people who can code, take analysis seriously, and want to build.

FAQ

Do I need experience?

Not for general membership. Committee work is organisational. The research department cares about programming and analytical thinking, not trading experience.

How much time does MQR require?

Membership is show up to what interests you. Committee is the work of running events, sponsors, and comms. Research is typically 2–10 hours a week.

Is MQR for Maastricht University students only?

Primarily, yes. Reach out if you are at a nearby institution.

Is any real money involved?

All trading is simulated on paper accounts. No real money is involved.
